Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

ctx is a local CLI tool. Your code never leaves your machine. No telemetry. No tracking. No cloud dependency.

What We Collect

Nothing from the CLI. The ctx CLI runs entirely on your machine. It does not:

  • Send your code, project structure, or any data to external servers
  • Require an account
  • Track usage or collect telemetry
  • Store any personal information

Future Services

If you choose to use future ctx.dev services (Pro tier, team features):

  • We will collect only the data necessary to provide the service
  • We will never access your source code
  • We will store your email, account preferences, and subscription status
  • You can delete your data at any time

Third-Party Services

The ctx CLI may use the following third-party services for distribution and hosting:

  • npm — Package distribution. See npm's privacy policy.
  • GitHub — Open-source repository hosting. See GitHub's privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare Pages — Website hosting. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Data Deletion

At any time, you can:

  • Delete your .ctx/ directory (local config)
  • Uninstall via npm uninstall -g ctxdotdev
  • Delete your ctx.dev account (when available)

Contact

Open an issue on GitHub or email security@ctx.dev.

# Privacy Policy for ctx

**Last updated: 2026-05-30**

## Our Commitment

ctx is a local CLI tool. We believe privacy is a feature.

## What We Collect

**Nothing from the CLI.** The ctx CLI runs entirely on your machine. It:
- Does NOT send your code, project structure, or any data to external servers
- Does NOT require an account
- Does NOT track usage
- Does NOT collect telemetry

## Future Services (ctx.dev SaaS)

If you choose to use future ctx.dev services (Pro tier, team features):
- We will collect only the data necessary to provide the service
- We will NOT access your source code
- We will store your email, account preferences, and subscription status
- You can delete your data at any time

## Third-Party Services

The ctx CLI may use the following:
- **npm/Homebrew:** Package distribution. See their privacy policies.
- **GitHub:** For open-source repository hosting. See GitHub's privacy policy.

## Data Deletion

At any time, you can:
- Delete your `.ctx/` directory (local config)
- Uninstall via `npm uninstall -g @ctx/cli`
- Delete your ctx.dev account (future feature)
